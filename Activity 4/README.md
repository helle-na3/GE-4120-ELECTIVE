### ✨ Overview & Description
> This activity involved delivering a **group presentation** on **Google Docs**, covering its core features, functions, advantages, disadvantages, and practical everyday applications for our classmates and professor. To make the session hands-on, we paired our discussion with a live demonstration and an interactive activity highlighting real-time collaboration and document-editing tools.

---

### 💡 What Have I Learned?

* **🤝 Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I deepened my technical understanding of Google Docs' features—particularly how real-time co-editing enables seamless multi-user collaboration. Beyond technical knowledge, I strengthened my teamwork, public speaking, and communication skills by collaborating with my groupmates and demonstrating digital workflows to our class.
* **🧩 Challenge & Solution:** One challenge our group encountered was structuring the presentation so that it was both comprehensive and engaging for the audience. We solved this by dividing the topics clearly among group members and incorporating an interactive activity to keep everyone actively involved.
* **🌱 Future Application & Growth:** I can apply these collaborative and presentation skills when preparing future academic requirements, co-authoring research papers, and explaining digital productivity tools clearly to peers or colleagues.
