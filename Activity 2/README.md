### ✨ Overview & Description
> This activity involved creating a **project documentation report** and an accompanying **infographic** focused on improving **emergency room referrals and patient transfers in the Philippines** through a digital referral system. While the written documentation detailed the current bottlenecks, their impact on healthcare delivery, and a proposed technological solution, the infographic distilled those key insights into an accessible, visual format.

---

### 💡 What Have I Learned?

* **🩺 Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how digital technology can solve real-world healthcare challenges by streamlining inter-hospital communication, documentation, and patient transfer coordination. I also strengthened my research, technical writing, and graphic design skills by translating a comprehensive report into a clear, visual summary.
* **🧩 Challenge & Solution:** One challenge I encountered was deciding which details to include in the infographic without overcrowding the layout. I solved this by prioritizing the most critical takeaways and using clean visual elements to keep the information easy to scan and understand.
* **🌱 Future Application & Growth:** I can apply these documentation and visual communication skills when developing future healthcare initiatives, designing patient or clinical educational materials, and exploring digital solutions that enhance patient care.
