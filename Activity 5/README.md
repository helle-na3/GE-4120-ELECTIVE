### ✨ Overview & Description
> This activity explored the use of **AI tools** in generating **hijab and abaya designs** through structured prompt engineering. I compared the results of a basic and simple prompt against an improved detailed prompt to observe how specific visual instructions directly influence AI-generated outputs.

---

### 💡 What Have I Learned?

* **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned that **prompt engineering** plays a vital role in generating accurate, creative, and detailed AI outputs. Providing specific instructions regarding colors, fabrics, silhouettes, and styling preferences produces significantly richer and more diverse design variations.
* **Challenge & Solution:** One challenge I encountered was that my initial, basic prompt generated repetitive and limited designs. I solved this by iteratively refining my prompt—layering in descriptive keywords, texture details, and specific aesthetic requirements.
* **Future Application & Growth:** I can apply this structured prompting skill in future creative, educational, and digital design projects while continuing to improve how I communicate clear visual ideas to AI tools.
