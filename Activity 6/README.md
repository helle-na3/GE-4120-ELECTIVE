### ✨ Overview & Description
> This activity involved building a one-page website for an advocacy project titled ***“Tawi-Tawi Unveiled: Discovering Islands, Preserving Heritage,”*** aimed at promoting the natural beauty, rich cultural heritage, and responsible tourism of Tawi-Tawi. Although the task originally specified Replit AI, I pivoted to **ChatGPT** after encountering technical limitations and paywalls—using detailed prompts to generate and customize my desired website content, layout, and design.

---

### 💡 What Have I Learned?

* **💻 Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how conversational AI tools like ChatGPT can assist in web development even without extensive coding background. I also strengthened my creativity, problem-solving, and prompt-engineering skills by structuring clear, detailed instructions to shape the site's layout and visual presentation.
* **🧩 Challenge & Solution:** One major challenge I faced was being unable to use Replit AI due to technical issues and paid feature restrictions, which I solved by resourcefully pivoting to ChatGPT to accomplish the project goals. Another challenge was aligning the generated code with my exact creative vision, which I addressed by iteratively refining my prompts and adjusting the design and content until I was satisfied.
* **🌱 Future Application & Growth:** This experience reinforced the value of adaptability and resourcefulness when working with emerging technologies. In the future, I can apply these skills to build more interactive websites, explore diverse AI tools, and continue deepening my understanding of web development.
