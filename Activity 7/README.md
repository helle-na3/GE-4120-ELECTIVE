### ✨ Overview & Description
> This activity involved using two large language models—**ChatGPT** and **Gemini**—to generate a professional one-page resume tailored for an **entry-level nursing position**. Using an identical prompt across both platforms, I compared their outputs based on structure, clarity, formatting, and overall quality to evaluate how different AI models interpret instructions and meet real-world professional standards.

---

### 💡 What Have I Learned?

* **🤖 Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how large language models can accelerate professional document creation and observed how the exact same prompt yields distinct stylistic and structural variations across different AI tools. I also strengthened my prompt-writing and critical evaluation skills by assessing both resumes for readability, organization, and visual presentation.
* **🧩 Challenge & Solution:** One challenge I encountered was deciding which AI-generated resume was more effective, as both models offered unique strengths. I solved this by systematically reviewing each draft side-by-side and prioritizing clean structure, clarity, and simplicity—reinforcing that human judgment and careful proofreading remain essential when using AI.
* **🌱 Future Application & Growth:** I can apply these comparative evaluation and prompt-refinement skills when preparing future nursing job applications, drafting career documents, and using AI tools responsibly and effectively in professional settings.
