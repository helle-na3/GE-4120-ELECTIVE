### ✨ Overview & Description
> This activity involved creating a **promotional poster** for **Booth 7 at Arrupe Hall**, advertising a **free coffee giveaway** scheduled for **August 4, 2026**. The design focused on capturing attention through creative typography, coffee-themed visuals, and an inviting color palette to communicate the event details clearly and effectively.

---

### 💡 What Have I Learned?

* **Key Concepts & Skills Gained:** I learned how to balance visual elements, typography, color harmony, and layout structure to create an engaging and informative poster. I also realized that effective graphic design must go beyond aesthetics—it needs to communicate the core message clearly to its target audience at a glance.
* **Challenge & Solution:** One challenge I encountered was arranging the event text and coffee graphics without making the poster feel cluttered or overcrowded. I solved this by establishing a clear visual hierarchy—organizing the layout cleanly and giving primary emphasis to the main promotional offer.
* **Future Application & Growth:** I can apply these layout and visual communication skills to future event promotions, academic presentations, and digital content while continuing to deepen my understanding of visual design principles.
